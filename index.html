Our Story

Our Farm

L & L Family Farm started pretty simply — Mark decided he wanted cows. But what started with a love for cattle quickly became something bigger: a desire to build a farm our children could grow up on, learn from, and hopefully have to call their own one day.

We’re a family farm in Wingate, North Carolina, raising commercial Angus and South Poll cattle. We’re not trying to be the biggest farm. We simply want to raise our cattle well, take care of the land we’ve been blessed with, and build something our children can be proud of for generations.

How We Raise Our Cattle

Good for the Cows.
Good for the Land.

We use rotational grazing to regularly move our cattle to fresh pasture. This gives sections of our pasture time to rest and recover while the cattle move on to fresh forage.

Over time, rotational grazing can help improve soil health, encourage stronger root systems, make better use of available forage, and naturally distribute nutrients across the pasture.

Most importantly, it gives our cattle access to fresh grass while allowing them to do what cattle were made to do — graze.

We believe healthier soil creates healthier pastures — and healthier pastures help us raise healthy, happy cattle.

Raised With Care

How our cattle are treated matters to us. They’re not just numbers in a pasture. We see them every day, we know them, and we do our best to give them a great life. We move them to fresh grass, make sure they have what they need, and treat them with the respect they deserve.

We don’t use growth hormones or pesticides, and we don’t routinely use antibiotics.

Our philosophy is simple: Take care of the land. Take care of the cattle. Leave both better than we found them.

Why We Farm

This was never just about owning cows. It’s about building something for our children.

We want them to grow up knowing the responsibility of caring for animals, the value of hard work, and the importance of taking care of the land we’ve been blessed with.

We’re still growing. We’re still learning. And we’re building something we hope will last for generations.
